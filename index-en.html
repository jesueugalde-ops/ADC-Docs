Is This for You?

How do I know if Human Innovation is for me?

You've done therapy. You've tried some wellness work and still you keep coming back to the same place. What you see outside is a mirror of an inner structure you haven't yet looked at head-on.

You may think you already know what the problem is: that relationship that isn't working — or any other bond you hold. That this is just who you are — this anxious, this explosive, this cold. That this depression, this anxiety, should have passed by now and you don't understand why they're still here. That this loss should already be healed. That this relationship is toxic. That stress and frustration are simply the price of the life you built. That these vices, these destructive habits, are just a pressure valve. That it's your team, their lack of ability or accountability. That it's the market, or your clients' limited buying power. Each one explains a part. None explains why it keeps repeating.

This isn't for you if...

  • You want someone else to tell you what to do instead of building your own judgment.
  • You expect magical results without being willing to transform what produces them.
  • You need the process to be comfortable all the time — here, what's uncomfortable gets named, with respect.

This is for you if...

  • You've achieved and you've lost; now, to achieve again, it's time to clarify what truly matters.
  • You're clear about where you want to go, but you lack the inner system to sustain the journey.
  • You recognize that your outer life is a mirror of an unresolved architecture, and you're ready to look at it.

If you saw yourself in any of this, the next step isn't another book or another weekend retreat. It's a 5-minute map and, if it makes sense to you, an hour with yourself, with someone who has already held this process with more than 2,000 people before you.

Common Questions

Details of the process and methodology.

What is centeredness?

It's the purpose behind the whole process: returning to your own center and deciding from there. Only you can reach and transform that center; I offer you a mirror and a structure to see it clearly. The past can't be rewritten, but the origin from which you decide can be redefined.

Does this replace psychological therapy?

No. This is human-development guidance and it doesn't replace therapy, which has its place. I support everyday-life topics such as anxiety, depression, addiction, self-esteem and guilt through self-knowledge and practice, without diagnosing. I don't work with severe personality disorders, autism, schizophrenia or psychopathy. Before we start I ask about your health and medication, and if what you're going through requires clinical care, I'll tell you clearly and suggest you lean on a mental health professional, alongside this work or before it.

How are family and partner relationships addressed in the process?

Our closest relationships can show us, like a mirror, patterns we carry from our origin: what we expect without saying it, what we inherit, when we rescue and when we control. We work on those bonds with real people and real dynamics: I guide you to see your part, without looking for culprits or burdening you with what isn't yours, and to decide what you want to keep and what you don't. If there is violence or risk, your safety comes first, with specialized support.

How does personal order connect with the performance of team members and teams?

The way a leader sustains themselves shows up in their team: with inner clarity it's easier to delegate, set boundaries and trust; with burnout, the team feels it. By working on your professional identity you organize how you decide, delegate and hold your boundaries, and that tends to transform what happens around you.

How long does it take to see tangible results in my day-to-day?

Change takes practice and time. Everyone moves at their own pace. The process is designed to produce noticeable signals from the first sessions; consolidating new patterns, where stability stops being forced and becomes natural, usually takes between 12 and 24 weeks, because a new pattern is consolidated through repetition. We'll measure it together so you can see it in your day-to-day.

Do I need prior experience in personal development or spirituality?

Not at all. The method adapts to your reality and your current vocabulary: we translate what may seem abstract into clear, structural tools that apply directly to your everyday professional, commercial and family challenges. The spiritual and the ancestral are an invitation, never a requirement.

How does payment work, and what if I need to pause or cancel?

You choose your plan: an upfront payment or weekly or monthly payments by transfer (I don't process card payments; if you need an invoice, let me know). You can reschedule with more than 24 hours' notice and pause for up to 2 weeks. If you need to cancel for financial reasons, that's not a problem. Guarantee: if your average satisfaction falls below 7.5/10 starting in week 4 and you completed 100% of your activities, agreements and commitments, I refund your investment and we discontinue the service.

Who can participate, and how many people do you guide at a time?

Ages 18 and older; in special cases, from 16 with a signed permission from a parent or guardian. Temazcales (sweat lodges) and ceremonies with medicinal plants are only for ages 18 and over and require a prior health and medication assessment. I guide up to 12 people at a time in individual processes.

What is the AI journal, and how do you protect my information?

You keep a daily journal with your own measurements. That information and your Miro canvas are analyzed with AI assistants that help me read your progress; the guidance and the decisions are mine. This is not 24/7 or crisis support. Before we start I ask for your written consent, I don't sell your data, and the details are in the privacy notice. If you're in crisis or at risk, call right away: 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline (U.S.) or Línea de la Vida (Mexico, 800 911 2000, 24 hours).
