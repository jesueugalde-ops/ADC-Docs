Is This for You?

How do I know if Human Innovation is for me?

You’ve already done therapy. You’ve already tried other ways to feel better. And you keep returning to the same place. What you see outside is the reflection of an inner structure you haven’t yet looked at head-on.

You probably already have an explanation:

  • A relationship that isn’t working“It’s toxic; that person is the problem.”

This is for you if…

  • You’ve achieved and you’ve lost, and to achieve again you need to get clear on what truly matters.
  • You know where you’re going, but you lack the inner system to sustain it.

Common Questions

What people usually ask before starting.

Does this replace psychological therapy?

No. This is personal and professional guidance, and it does not replace therapy, which has its place. I work on decisions, identity, relationships, habits and leadership. I don’t diagnose or treat any condition, and I never advise on medication; that is always between you and your doctor. Before we start I ask a few questions about your health and who supports you. If what you’re going through needs clinical care, I’ll tell you clearly, help you find a licensed professional and, when it makes sense and with your consent, work in parallel with them.

What happens in a Mapping Session?

In 60 minutes you read your 12-area radar and see how one area pushes on another. You draw yourself for two minutes and we look at it together, as a mirror, not as a test. We name what you see, without judgment, and you leave with a proposal that fits your time and budget.

How long does it take to see tangible results in my day-to-day?

Change takes practice and everyone moves at their own pace. You’ll notice perceptible signs from the first sessions; consolidation, when the new stops feeling forced and becomes natural, usually takes 12 to 24 weeks, because a new pattern is consolidated through repetition. We measure it together so you can see it in your day to day.

How does payment work, and what if I need to pause or cancel?

You choose your plan: pay in full or in weekly or monthly payments, by card (Stripe), Wise, PayPal or bank transfer. If you need an invoice, tell me. You can reschedule with more than 24 hours’ notice and pause for up to 2 weeks. If you need to cancel for financial reasons, that’s not a problem. Guarantee: the full conditions are in Ways to work together.

Do I need prior experience in personal development or spirituality?

No. The method adapts to your reality and your vocabulary: we translate the abstract into clear tools you can apply to your professional, commercial and family challenges. The spiritual and the ancestral are an invitation, never a requirement.

What if I’m not someone who talks about my emotions?

You don’t have to be. We start from what you do (decisions, relationships, work), and the words come with practice, at your pace.

How are family and partner relationships addressed in the process?

Our closest relationships can show us, like a mirror, patterns we carry from our origin: what we expect without saying, what we inherited, when we rescue and when we control. We work on those bonds with real people and real dynamics: I help you see your part, without looking for culprits or loading you with what isn’t yours, and decide what you want to keep and what you don’t. If there is violence or risk, your safety comes first, with specialized support.

What is centeredness?

It is the purpose behind the whole process: returning to your own center and deciding from there. Only you can reach and transform that center; I offer you a mirror and a structure to see it clearly. The past isn’t rewritten, but the origin you decide from can be redefined.

What is the AI journal, and how do you protect my information?

You keep a daily journal with your own measurements. That information and your Miro canvas are analyzed with AI assistants that help me read your progress; the guidance and the decisions are mine. This is not 24/7 care or crisis support. Before we start I ask for your written consent, I don’t sell your data, and the details are in the privacy notice. If you are in crisis or at risk, call 988 (U.S.) or the Línea de la Vida (Mexico, 800 911 2000, 24 hours) right away.

Is what I share with you private?

Yes. I keep what you share private, and I don’t sell it or share it for commercial purposes; the details are in the privacy notice. Because this is guidance and not therapy, it isn’t covered by the legal confidentiality that exists between a patient and a licensed therapist. If you have questions about that, ask me before we start.

Do you offer ceremonies?

Not as part of the program. If you are curious about the ancestral traditions I have learned in Mexico, we can talk about them, but they are separate, optional, held in Mexico with the elders who guide them, for adults 18 and older only with a prior health and medication review, and never presented as treatment. Nobody should feel pressure to take part.

Who can participate, and how many people do you guide at a time?

Adults 18 and older. I accompany up to 12 people at a time in one-on-one processes.
