Common Questions

What people usually ask before starting.

Does this replace psychological therapy?

No. This is human-development guidance and it does not replace therapy, which has its place. I accompany everyday issues such as anxiety, depression, addictions, self-esteem and guilt through self-knowledge and practice, without diagnosing. I do not work with severe personality disorders, autism, schizophrenia or psychopathy. Before we start I ask about your health and medication, and if what you’re going through requires clinical care, I’ll tell you clearly and suggest you work with a mental health professional, in parallel or beforehand.

How long does it take to see tangible results in my day-to-day?

Change takes practice and everyone moves at their own pace. You’ll notice perceptible signs from the first sessions; consolidation, when the new stops feeling forced and becomes natural, usually takes 12 to 24 weeks, because a new pattern is consolidated through repetition. We measure it together so you can see it in your day to day.

How does payment work, and what if I need to pause or cancel?

You choose your plan: upfront payment or weekly or monthly bank transfers (I don’t handle cards; if you need an invoice, tell me). You can reschedule with more than 24 hours’ notice and pause for up to 2 weeks. If you need to cancel for financial reasons, that’s not a problem. Guarantee: if your average satisfaction falls below 7.5/10 starting in week 4 and you completed 100% of your activities, agreements and commitments, I will refund your investment and we will discontinue the service.

Do I need prior experience in personal development or spirituality?

No. The method adapts to your reality and your vocabulary: we translate the abstract into clear tools you can apply to your professional, commercial and family challenges. The spiritual and the ancestral are an invitation, never a requirement.

How are family and partner relationships addressed in the process?

Our closest relationships can show us, like a mirror, patterns we carry from our origin: what we expect without saying, what we inherited, when we rescue and when we control. We work on those bonds with real people and real dynamics: I help you see your part, without looking for culprits or loading you with what isn’t yours, and decide what you want to keep and what you don’t. If there is violence or risk, your safety comes first, with specialized support.

How does personal order connect with the performance of team members and teams?

How a leader holds themselves shows in their team: with inner clarity it’s easier to delegate, set limits and trust; with exhaustion, the team feels it. When you work on your professional identity, how you decide changes, and that usually transforms what happens around you.

What is centeredness?

It is the purpose behind the whole process: returning to your own center and deciding from there. Only you can reach and transform that center; I offer you a mirror and a structure to see it clearly. The past isn’t rewritten, but the origin you decide from can be redefined.

What is the AI journal, and how do you protect my information?

You keep a daily journal with your own measurements. That information and your Miro canvas are analyzed with AI assistants that help me read your progress; the guidance and the decisions are mine. This is not 24/7 care or crisis support. Before we start I ask for your written consent, I don’t sell your data, and the details are in the privacy notice. If you are in crisis or at risk, call 988 (U.S.) or the Línea de la Vida (Mexico, 800 911 2000, 24 hours) right away.

Who can participate, and how many people do you guide at a time?

Adults 18 and older; in special cases, from age 16 with signed permission from a parent or guardian. Temazcal (sweat lodge) and ceremonies with medicinal plants are for people 18 and older only and require a prior health and medication review. I accompany up to 12 people at a time in one-on-one processes.
